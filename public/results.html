    Reading these numbers

    • Compare weeks, not days. A small store’s daily figures jump around: one order more or less moves a day’s rate a lot.
    • Weekends usually bring more visits, so a fair comparison covers whole weeks either side.
    • A difference this size takes a few weeks to be sure of, and other things change too (a sale, an ad, the season).
