Where it works

Shopify only, on purpose.

Every Shopify store publishes its pages and product details, so QuickLift can check yours with nothing to install and no login.

When you approve a change, it goes into your theme as one small snippet. Undo empties that snippet, so it can never wipe out an edit you made yourself.

Questions in the margin

Will shoppers see a change before I approve it?
No. You see it first, on a copy of your own page. QuickLift never publishes on its own.
Do I need a developer?
No. Switch on a ready-made fix, or describe what you want in plain words. The agent writes the change and shows it to you on your page.
Will it make things up?
No. It never adds fake reviews, countdown timers or discounts. If a change needs a fact only you know, like your returns window, it leaves it for you to fill in.
What does the free check need from me?
Just your store’s address. It reads the pages any shopper can see.
Can I take a change back?
Yes. Undo takes QuickLift’s changes off your store in one click.
