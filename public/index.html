Questions

Will shoppers see a change before I approve it?
No. You see it first, on a copy of your own page. QuickLift never publishes on its own.
Do I need a developer?
No. Switch on a ready-made fix, or describe what you want in plain words. The agent writes the change and shows it to you on your page.
Will it make things up?
No. It never adds fake reviews, countdown timers or discounts. If a change needs a fact only you know, like your returns window, it leaves it for you to fill in.
Can I take a change back?
Yes. Undo takes QuickLift’s changes off your store in one click, without touching anything else in your theme.
Why not just run an A/B test?
A test needs lots of visitors before it tells you anything, so a small store can wait months. QuickLift starts from changes that published research already backs.
Does it work on my store?
If it’s on Shopify, yes. Every Shopify store’s pages are public, so there’s nothing to install and no login for the check.
What does it cost?
Nothing while we test it with a few Australian stores.
