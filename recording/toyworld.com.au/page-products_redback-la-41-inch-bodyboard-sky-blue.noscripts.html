Description

Take on the waves with the Redback Surf 41" Little Avalon Performance Bodyboard, designed for riders who want speed, control and plenty of fun. Its durable construction and high-buoyancy EPS core provide stability and performance for a range of wave conditions.

Key Features:

  • 41" performance bodyboard: Designed for speed, control and durability
  • XPE deck: Provides comfort, durability and impact resistance
  • Thermo-bond construction: Helps prevent bubbling in Australia's harsh conditions
  • EPS core: Lightweight, rigid and highly buoyant
  • Slick bottom: Helps improve speed, strength and durability
  • Twin channels: Direct water flow for improved speed and grip
  • Includes wrist leash and plug: Ready for your next wave
  • Lifetime warranty: Covers manufacturing, material and workmanship faults, excluding misuse and fair wear and tear

Suitable For Ages 10+
